Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

In short: to show the season's Top 100, Street Rivals saves a nickname and your ranked rank and points on our leaderboard server. We don't ask for your real name, email, age or location, the game has no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies of its own (any ads you see come from the site you play on), and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Street Rivals is a browser game made by an independent developer. For anything about this policy or your data, email aenestoy@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

On our leaderboard server, only after you race a ranked stage (or choose a nickname):

  • A nickname (3–16 characters). If you haven't chosen one, the server gives you one like "Racer1234".
  • Your ranked rank and points for each season and round, and when they last changed.
  • A one-way hash of a random device key the game makes on your device (the key itself is not stored on the server).
  • For limits against abuse: the time of your last score send, and when a new player registers, a one-way hash of the IP address with the time (deleted after about 1 day). The IP address itself is not stored in our database.

Your nickname, rank and points are public: every player can see them in the Top 100. Please don't use your real name or any personal information as a nickname.

On your device: your progress, cars, parts, settings, the device key and your nickname are kept in your browser's local storage. On CrazyGames a copy of your progress is also kept in the portal's own cloud save.

What we don't collect: real names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. The game sets no cookies of its own.

3. Why

  • To show the season's Top 100 and your place in it.
  • To keep the board fair: checking that ranks and points are possible, blocking offensive nicknames, and limiting how often scores and new players can be sent.

4. Where it is stored

Our leaderboard database runs on Supabase (our hosting provider) in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Like any web service, it handles your IP address to answer the game's requests and may keep short technical logs under its own terms. When the game can't reach the server, everything stays on your device and the game works as before.

5. How long we keep it

  • Nickname, ranks and points: while the leaderboard runs, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Hashed IP records: about 1 day.
  • Data on your device: until you clear your browser's site data for the game.

6. Your choices

  • Change your nickname any time in the Top 100 screen.
  • To delete your leaderboard data, email us your nickname (and, if you can, the season you raced in).
  • Children: the game does not ask for personal information. A nickname should never be a real name.

7. Terms of use

Play fair: don't cheat, don't send fake scores, and don't use offensive nicknames. We may remove scores and nicknames that break these rules. The game is provided as it is, for fun, without warranties.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
