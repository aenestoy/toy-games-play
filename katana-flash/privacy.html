Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: Katana Flash has no accounts, no nicknames and no online leaderboards. Your progress stays on your device (and in the game site's own cloud save where it has one). The game counts play only anonymously and sends short error reports when something breaks (see “Anonymous play statistics”). We don’t ask for your name, email, age or location, the game has no advertising or tracking cookies of its own (any ads you see come from the site you play on), and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

Katana Flash is a browser game made by an independent developer. For anything about this policy, email aenestoy@gmail.com.

2. What data the game handles

On your device

Your progress (stages cleared and stars, gold, ink stones, village buildings, katanas and costumes, your Dojo best and today’s daily challenge best, when you last opened the daily chest) and the sound setting are kept in your browser’s local storage. On sites that offer a cloud save (for example CrazyGames and Yandex Games) a copy is kept there too, so it can follow your account on that site.

Anonymous play statistics

On CrazyGames and on our own site, while the game is open and in front, it sends a small anonymous note to our server about once a minute, so we can see how many people are playing and which parts of the game they use. The note holds: a random install number made on your device (not linked to you or to any account), a random number for this visit, the game and version, the site you play on, your language, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and browser family (such as Chrome), the screen you are on, how many seconds you spent on each screen, and a few game steps (for example “first cut”, “stage 3 cleared”, “village opened”, “daily challenge played”). Our server may add a two-letter country code (such as TR) from its network provider; your IP address is not stored for this. No names, no email, no precise location, no fingerprinting.

If something in the game goes wrong (a program error the game did not expect), it also sends a short error report, at most five per visit: the error message and the place in the game’s code, the visit number (not the install number), the game and version, the site, the kind of device and browser family, what the game was doing (for example “playing stage 5”) and how long the page had been open. Web addresses, email addresses and long codes are removed from the message before it is sent.

Automated browsers and the Poki, Yandex Games and Playgama versions of the game send nothing.

What we don’t collect

No real names, nicknames, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. The game sets no cookies of its own and contains no advertising code of its own; its only statistics are the anonymous play statistics above.

3. Why we use it

  • Your progress: so you can continue where you left off.
  • Anonymous play statistics and error reports: to see how many people play, where new players stop, whether a new version plays better, and to fix errors.

4. Where it is stored

The anonymous statistics and error reports go to our database on Supabase (our hosting provider) in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Like any web service, Supabase handles your IP address to deliver the requests and may keep short technical logs under its own terms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Anonymous play statistics: the per-visit notes about 2 days, the game steps 30 days, which days an install number played 35 days, and its first and last day of play (for return rates) until it has not played for about 400 days. Daily totals (counts and minutes, without install numbers) are kept. Error reports are kept 30 days; daily error counts (without visit numbers) about 400 days.
  • Data on your device: until you clear your browser’s site data for the game (or the site deletes its cloud save).

6. Who we share it with

Nobody. We don’t sell, rent or give data to anyone, and we don’t use it for advertising. Supabase stores the statistics for us as our hosting provider.

The game sites where you play Katana Flash (such as CrazyGames, Poki and Yandex Games) run their own accounts, ads, cookies and analytics. They process that data themselves, under their own privacy policies; please read those as well.

7. Children

Katana Flash is meant for players aged 12 and over (PEGI 12). The game never asks for personal or contact details, and its statistics are anonymous.

8. Your rights

We hold nothing that names you. You can remove your progress at any time by clearing the game’s site data in your browser. For any question or request, email aenestoy@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Cihazında

Bulut kaydı olan sitelerde (örneğin CrazyGames ve Yandex Games) bir kopyası orada da tutulur; böylece o sitedeki hesabınla seni izler.

Anonim oyun istatistikleri

CrazyGames’te ve kendi sitemizde, oyun açık ve öndeyken, yaklaşık dakikada bir sunucumuza küçük ve anonim bir not gider; böylece kaç kişinin oynadığını ve oyunun hangi bölümlerinin kullanıldığını görürüz. Notta şunlar vardır: cihazında üretilen rastgele bir kurulum numarası (sana ya da bir hesaba bağlı değildir), bu ziyaret için rastgele bir numara, oyun ve sürümü, oynadığın site, dilin, cihaz türü (telefon, tablet ya da bilgisayar) ve tarayıcı ailesi (Chrome gibi), bulunduğun ekran, her ekranda kaç saniye geçtiği ve birkaç oyun adımı (örneğin "ilk kesiş", "3. bölüm geçildi", "köy açıldı", "günün görevi oynandı"). Sunucumuz, ağ sağlayıcısından gelen iki harfli ülke kodunu (TR gibi) ekleyebilir; bunun için IP adresin saklanmaz. Ad, e-posta, kesin konum ya da parmak izi yoktur.

Oyunda bir şey ters giderse (oyunun beklemediği bir program hatası), ziyaret başına en çok beş kısa hata raporu da gider: hata metni ve oyun kodundaki yeri, ziyaret numarası (kurulum numarası değil), oyun ve sürümü, site, cihaz türü ve tarayıcı ailesi, oyunun o an ne yaptığı (örneğin "5. bölüm oynanıyor") ve sayfanın ne kadar süredir açık olduğu. Göndermeden önce metindeki web adresleri, e-posta adresleri ve uzun kodlar silinir.

Otomatik tarayıcılar ile oyunun Poki, Yandex Games ve Playgama sürümleri hiçbir şey göndermez.

Neleri toplamıyoruz

Gerçek ad, takma ad, e-posta adresi, şifre, telefon numarası, kesin konum, kişiler, fotoğraf ya da ödeme bilgisi toplamayız. Oyun kendine ait çerez koymaz ve kendine ait reklam kodu içermez; tek istatistiği yukarıdaki anonim oyun istatistikleridir.
