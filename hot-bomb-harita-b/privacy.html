Hot Bomb

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Hot Bomb has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email, age or location. Your progress is saved in your browser (and in your CrazyGames account when you are signed in). The game counts play only anonymously, and it reports open rooms (without names) so we can see how many rooms are running. Online play connects your device to the other players' devices, directly or through a relay server, so they can see a network address. The game has no advertising or tracking cookies of its own, and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

Hot Bomb is a browser game made by an independent developer. For anything about this policy, email aenestoy@gmail.com.

2. What data the game handles

On your device and in your CrazyGames account

Your progress (coins, level and XP, league trophies, the items you own and wear in the Locker, daily reward days) and your sound setting are kept in your browser's local storage. On CrazyGames a copy is also kept in CrazyGames' own save for your account, so it can follow you to another device.

Your name in rooms

If you are signed in to CrazyGames, the game shows your CrazyGames username to the other players in your room; it gets that name from CrazyGames. If you are not signed in, the game makes up a random name for each visit. Your name, character, cosmetics and league badge are sent only to the players in the same room, for the duration of the match. We don't store them on our server.

Online play

To play together, the game connects the devices in a room directly to each other (WebRTC). To find a route, it asks public connection helpers (STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare), and when a direct connection is not possible it passes the game data through a relay server (Cloudflare TURN). Our server (Supabase) helps the devices find each other, lists open public rooms (a room code, how many players and seats, a rough region taken from your device's time zone such as "Europe", and the game version) and, for rooms with friends, can pass game messages along. As with any connection over the internet, the other players' devices, these helpers and the relay server can see your network (IP) address. We don't store IP addresses for this.

The game has no free text chat: players can only send six fixed emoji, and they are turned off when CrazyGames' chat setting is off.

Anonymous play statistics

On CrazyGames, while the game is open and in front, it sends a small anonymous note to our server about once a minute, so we can see how many people are playing and which parts of the game they use. The note holds: a random install number made on your device (not linked to you or to any account), a random number for this visit, the game and version, the site you play on, your language, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and browser family (such as Chrome), the screen you are on, how many seconds you spent on each screen, how many seconds were actually played, how long the page took to load and to start the first match, and how many messages the page exchanged with our server. Our server may add a two-letter country code (such as TR) from its network provider; your IP address is not stored for this. No names, no email, no precise location, no fingerprinting.

Room reports

The player who opens a room sends short reports while the room is open: a random room number (not the invite code), whether it is a public or a friends' room, how many players and bots are in it, the arena, a rough region, the game version, how busy the room is and whether a match is running. These reports hold no names and are only used to see how many rooms are open.

What we don't collect

No real names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. The game sets no cookies of its own and contains no advertising code of its own.

3. Why we use it

  • Your progress: so you can continue where you left off.
  • Online play data: so the players in a room can see and play with each other.
  • Anonymous play statistics and room reports: to see how many people play, whether rooms fill up and whether a new version plays better.

4. Where it is stored

The anonymous statistics go to our database on Supabase (our hosting provider) in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Room listings, room messages and room reports go through Supabase's real-time service in Singapore and are not stored in a database. Like any web service, Supabase and Cloudflare handle your IP address to deliver the requests and may keep short technical logs under their own terms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Anonymous play statistics: the per-visit notes about 2 days, game steps 30 days, which days an install number played 35 days, and its first and last day of play until it has not played for about 400 days. Daily totals (without install numbers) are kept.
  • Room listings and room reports: only while the room is open.
  • Data on your device: until you clear your browser's site data for the game (or delete it from your CrazyGames account).

6. Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or give data to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising. Supabase and Cloudflare handle data for us as our service providers. The players in your room see your name, character and badge while you play together.

CrazyGames runs its own accounts, ads, cookies and analytics. It processes that data itself, under its own privacy policy; please read it as well.

7. Children

Hot Bomb is meant for players aged 12 and over (PEGI 12). The game never asks for personal or contact details, has no free text chat, and its statistics are anonymous.

8. Your rights

We hold nothing that names you. You can remove your progress at any time by clearing the game's site data in your browser. For any question or request, email aenestoy@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
